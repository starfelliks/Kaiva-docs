Privacy Policy

Kaiva is a calorie and nutrition tracker for Android. This page explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and the choices you have. In short: Kaiva has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and your diary stays on your device unless you choose to use an online feature.

Effective: November 9, 2026 Last updated: November 9, 2026 Version 1.0

At a glance

  • No account, no sign-up. We do not ask for your email, phone number or password.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. No advertising ID, no third-party analytics, no profiling.
  • Your data stays on your device. Profile, diary, water, weight, custom foods and settings are stored locally.
  • Online features are optional and narrow. Barcode lookup sends only the barcode; AI photo analysis sends the photo plus basic technical data.
  • You stay in control. Delete entries in the app, clear its data, or uninstall it. Server-side quota records expire within 48 hours.

01 Who we are

Kaiva is an Android application with the package name com.kaiva.calorietracker, developed by Starfelliks, an independent developer. For the purposes of applicable data protection law, Starfelliks is the data controller for the limited processing described on this page.

This policy applies to the Kaiva app. It does not apply to websites, apps or services operated by third parties, even when the app links to them.

For the rules that apply to your use of the app, see our Terms of Use.

02 Data stored on your device

Kaiva is designed to work primarily offline. The information you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive it, and it is not synchronized to our servers.

This includes:

  • your optional display name and avatar;
  • gender, age, height, weight, goal, activity level and weekly pace;
  • daily calorie, protein, fat, carbohydrate and water targets, including manual overrides;
  • diary entries, meals, portion sizes and nutritional values;
  • custom foods and products you create;
  • water intake and weight history;
  • measurement system, theme, interface language and other app settings;
  • local counters for the daily AI scan limit.

The app uses this information only to calculate your targets, display your diary and charts, and generate reports.

Permissions

The app requests two Android permissions: Internet and Camera. Camera access is optional and is used only while a scanner screen is open. Kaiva does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone, SMS, calendar, or photo library, and there is no background camera use.

Device backups. If backup is enabled on your Android device, the operating system may include app data in the backup of your Google account. This is controlled by your Android and Google settings, not by Kaiva. You can review or delete backups in your Google account settings.

03 Camera and photos

Barcode scanning

When you scan a barcode, the camera image is processed on your device by Google ML Kit. The image is not saved and is not sent to us. Only the decoded barcode number and standard technical request headers are sent to Open Food Facts to look up product information (see Barcode lookups).

AI photo analysis

If you choose the optional AI photo analysis feature, the app captures a JPEG image and sends it over HTTPS to our API, which is hosted on Cloudflare. Our API forwards the image to DeepSeek for analysis. We do not save the photo on our servers, and we do not use it to train AI models. The result — food names, estimated portions and nutritional values — is returned to your device and stored only if you choose to add it to your diary.

Photos can contain more than food. Please do not photograph people's faces, documents, screens or anything else you do not want to send to a third-party AI provider.

You can use Kaiva without granting camera access. Barcode scanning and AI photo analysis are the only features that need the camera.

04 AI photo analysis: technical data

When the app sends a photo for analysis, it also sends a small set of technical data needed to operate the feature securely:

Random installation ID
Generated on first use and stored in the app's private storage. It is not derived from your device's hardware identifiers, and a new one is created after you reinstall the app or clear its data. It is used to apply the daily scan limit and to prevent abuse.
App version and interface language
Used to format the analysis result in your language.
Local date
Used to apply the daily limit in your time zone.
Request signature
Used to verify that the request comes from the Kaiva app.
Google Play Integrity token (if enabled)
Used to check that the app is genuine and has not been modified.
IP address
Received by our infrastructure provider when the request reaches the server. It is used for rate limiting and is stored only as a truncated hash for quota purposes.

These requests do not include your name, email address, contacts, precise location or advertising ID.

Our API stores counters for the installation ID and the hashed IP address for up to 48 hours so that daily limits work. Cloudflare, as our infrastructure provider, may also process technical request metadata (such as IP address, timestamps and error codes) in logs for security and reliability, under its own privacy policy.

05 Barcode lookups

If a scanned barcode is not found in the app's built-in database, Kaiva may query Open Food Facts, a public food database. The request contains the barcode number and standard technical headers, including an app identifier. It does not include your profile, diary or any other content from the app.

Open Food Facts is an independent project. Its handling of request data is described in its own privacy policy.

06 Reports and sharing

Reports are generated on your device as PDF files. Nothing is uploaded to us. If you tap Share, Android passes the file to the app you choose. That app's privacy policy applies to anything you share with it.

07 Payments

Kaiva does not currently process payments. If paid features become available, purchases and subscriptions will be handled by Google Play Billing. We will not receive or store your payment card details. Google's privacy policy applies to payment processing.

09 What we do not collect

  • No account, email address, phone number or password.
  • No contacts, calendar, SMS or call logs.
  • No precise or background location.
  • No microphone access.
  • No advertising identifier and no cross-app tracking.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or session recording.
  • No access to your photo library or files, other than the camera frame you capture.
  • No sale or rental of personal data.

The optional display name you may enter in your profile is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.

10 How we use data

We use information only for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Providing the app's features. Calculations, diary, charts and reports are produced on your device from the data you enter.
  • Optional AI photo analysis. The photo and technical data are processed only when you choose to use this feature. You can withdraw this consent by not using the feature.
  • Security and abuse prevention. Request signatures, rate limits, quota counters and, where enabled, Play Integrity help protect the service from misuse.
  • Legal compliance. We may process data if required by applicable law.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are the performance of the service you request, your consent for the optional AI scan, and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure. Where other laws apply, we process data only as described on this page.

11 International transfers

Cloudflare operates a global network. DeepSeek is based in China. Google is based in the United States and operates globally. When you use the optional online features, your photo and technical data may be processed in these countries. Data protection laws in those countries may differ from the laws where you live.

We limit the data sent to what is described in this policy and rely on the safeguards published by these providers in their own privacy policies. If you do not want your photo to be processed outside your country, simply do not use the AI photo analysis feature.

12 Retention and deletion

Data on your device
Kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can delete individual diary entries, weight records and custom foods in the app.
AI scan photos
Not stored by us after the analysis response is returned. DeepSeek's own retention practices are described in its privacy policy.
Quota counters
Installation ID and hashed IP counters expire automatically within 48 hours.
Server logs
Limited technical metadata may be kept by our infrastructure provider for security and troubleshooting, in line with its settings and policies.
Device backups
If Android backup is enabled, a copy of app data may remain in your Google account after you uninstall the app, until it is removed there or expires. We cannot delete it for you.
Messages you send us
Kept only as long as needed to respond, then deleted.

How to delete your data

  • Delete entries, weight records or custom foods directly in the app.
  • To remove everything on the device, open Android Settings → Apps → Kaiva → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local storage. Server-side quota records expire automatically within 48 hours.
  • For any remaining request, contact us at the address in Contact.

13 Security

Network requests use HTTPS/TLS. Requests to our AI scan API are signed, rate-limited and, where enabled, checked with Google Play Integrity. The app has no accounts or passwords to protect, and photos are processed in memory rather than saved to storage.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe there is a security problem, please contact us so we can investigate.

14 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to receive a portable copy, and to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Most of your data is on your device, so you can exercise these rights directly: view and edit your profile, delete entries, or clear the app's data. Because we do not have accounts, we often cannot identify you in our server records. If you contact us about the limited server-side data, we will explain what we can and cannot do and will respond within 30 days.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, we do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not knowingly collect personal information from consumers under 16.

15 Children

Kaiva is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The age you enter, if any, is used only for calorie calculations and is stored on your device.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent a photo or otherwise provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate action.
